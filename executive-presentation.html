Commercial Strategy

Key Pricing Levers

Modules commanding premium rates due to complexity, integration depth, or strategic value

🤖
AI CV Screening (1.3)
Proprietary trained ML model for CV parsing, NLP-based skill extraction, 0–100 candidate fit scoring, and applicant ranking engine. Custom-trained AI commands the highest per-module premium in the market.
HIGHEST PREMIUM
💳
Payment Disbursement (1.10)
3-channel disbursement — M-Pesa, Airtel Money, Bank Transfer — all backed by IntaSend. Each channel requires certification, sandbox testing, and compliance documentation. IntaSend provides the secure settlement layer.
HIGHEST PREMIUM
🏗️
Multi-Tenant Architecture (4.2)
Multi-company Supabase Postgres with RLS on every table, Vault encryption for PII, automated cron jobs. Significant upfront architecture cost — not an afterthought feature.
ARCHITECTURE PREMIUM
📱
Employee PWA (App 2)
A fully separate application — offline-first architecture, service worker, install prompt, app manifest, mobile-optimised UX. Typically priced as a standalone product line by engineering firms.
SEPARATE PRODUCT LINE
🛡️
Compliance & Audit (1.15 + 4.3)
KDPA 2019 compliance, 7-year audit retention, CSP headers, soft-delete-only policy, rate limiting. Regulatory compliance engagements require specialist review and legal alignment — billed separately.
COMPLIANCE PREMIUM
🌍
i18n — Swahili (2.7)
Additional language support via a shared package (en.json / sw.json) across Dashboard and PWA. Internationalisation is always an add-on module — priced per additional language pair.
